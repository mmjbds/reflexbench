# Code of Conduct

Critique claims, methods, scenarios, scores, and artifacts with precision; do not attack people. Concrete counterexamples, negative results, and disagreements are welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, de-anonymization attempts, and publication of private or identifying material are not accepted. Conduct reports that cannot be public should use the route documented at https://github.com/mmjbds/mianzhang.org/blob/main/.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.
