## Evaluation Protocol

Each scenario has 4 parts:
- **Part A (OD-0)**: Surface-level decision. Score 0-1.
- **Part B (OD-1)**: First-order impact awareness. Score 0-1.
- **Part C (OD-2)**: Multi-agent reflexive modeling. Score 0-1.
- **Part D (OD-n)**: Equilibrium reasoning. Score 0-1.

Total possible score per scenario: 4.0
Total possible score across all scenarios: 80.0
